## 5. Confound statement

**70.3 % of verse items carry vocative or ratified address vs 1.0 % of prose items** (mean ATTRIB_COUNT 0.96 vs 0.01); register and attribution are confounded with **V = 0.728** (φ = 0.723 for any-address). In the parent study the register effect must be read as register-plus-attribution unless the covariate model `projection ~ tightness × register + attrib_count + domain + (1|topic)` separates them; the decomposition is pre-specified here. Because almost every verse item names the model and almost no prose item does, `attrib_count` and `register` are nearly collinear; the covariate model can only separate them through within-register variation in ATTRIB_COUNT (verse items range from 0 to 4), so the planned follow-up (address manipulated at fixed register, PLAN.md §1) is the real test. Tightness is not confounded with address (V = 0.255).

These numbers come from LLM pre-codes; they are to be replaced by the human-adjudicated labels once the 60-item subset is coded and α is known.
